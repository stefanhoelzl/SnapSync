How it works

Three steps. No account.

01

Scan the event QR

Point your camera at the host's code to join. That's the whole sign-up.

02

Your photos flow in

Only pictures taken after you join go to the event. Screenshots and saved memes stay out.

03

Everyone's arrive in your library

The whole group's photos of the moment land right in your camera roll. No shared album to check.

Private by design

Your camera roll stays yours.

SnapSync was built so a guest can share the moment without handing over their photo library. It shares only what belongs to the event — and nothing about you.

No account

No email, no password, no profile. You join an event by scanning it.

Anonymous by design

Your device is a random ID — never your name, number, or contacts.

Event-scoped

Photos are shared to that one event and reach only the people in it.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no cookies. This site sets nothing on your device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 July 2026 · Contact: stefanh+snapsync@posteo.de

SnapSync ("we", "the app") is an independent project that lets people share the photos from an event with each other. This policy explains what data the app processes and why. We designed SnapSync to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts.

Who is responsible

The data controller for SnapSync is the project's operator, reachable at stefanh+snapsync@posteo.de. Please use this address for any privacy question or request.

What we process, and why

  • Photos you share. When you join an event, photos you take afterwards are uploaded so the other people in that event can receive them. They are stored to make sharing work, and are removed when the event ends. Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • A random device ID. Each install generates a random identifier so the service can tell devices apart within an event. It is not linked to your identity and we cannot use it to contact you.
  • A push token. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a token so we can tell your device when new photos are ready. It is used only for that.
  • An app-integrity check. The app proves to our server that it is a genuine, unmodified copy of SnapSync (Apple App Attest). This checks the app, not you, and carries no personal information. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind — in the app or on this website.

Who processes it for us

We rely on two service providers to run SnapSync:

  • Bunny.net — hosting, storage, and content delivery for the photos and the service.
  • Apple — push notifications, the app-integrity check, and app distribution.

How long we keep photos

Photos are stored only to enable sharing within an event. When an event ends, its photos are deleted. If you want data associated with your device removed sooner, email us at the address above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no name or email for you, we may need your device's identifier to act on a request — email us and we'll help.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
